About

Entrepreneurship is learned by doing. VyavasAI gives every first-timer a whole founding team — before they risk a single dollar.

Why it exists

Most startup advice is either a motivational poster or a 40-slide deck. Neither tells you the one thing you need to hear: where your idea is weak.

So we built a room. A supervisor assembles the right specialists, they debate your idea across rounds, a chair keeps them honest, and a judge grades the result. It runs on a LangGraph workflow with Django — built for the Imagine Cup.

Everything happens in plain language. If a ten-year-old cannot follow the debate, we consider it a bug.
