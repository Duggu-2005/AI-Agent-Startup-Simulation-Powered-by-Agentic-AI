The process

From raw idea to a scored verdict in four moves.

No spreadsheets, no business jargon. You describe the idea in your own words and a team of AI specialists argues it out until a judge can grade it honestly.

01

Tell us your idea

Four plain-words answers: what you are building, who it helps, how it earns, and why it is different.

The agents debate in rounds

The judge delivers a verdict
